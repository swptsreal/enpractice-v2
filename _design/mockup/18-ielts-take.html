The Return of the Urban Bee

Reading Passage 1 · khoảng 900 từ

A For most of the twentieth century, the city was assumed to be hostile territory for pollinators. Concrete, traffic and a scarcity of flowering plants were thought to make urban environments a net loss for insect diversity. That assumption has been quietly overturned over the past fifteen years.

B Surveys conducted across a dozen European cities found that certain bee species are not merely surviving in urban areas but thriving there, in some cases at higher densities than in the surrounding farmland. The explanation appears to lie in two factors: the diversity of garden planting, and the absence of the monoculture farming that dominates rural land.

C The finding is not universally encouraging, however. The species that benefit tend to be generalists, able to feed on many plant types. Specialist species, which depend on a single plant family, have continued to decline in cities as they have elsewhere.
